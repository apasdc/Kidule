Privacy Policy — Kidule

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Kidule helps you track extracurricular classes — their schedule, attendance and fees. Your privacy is simple to explain because the app keeps everything on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts and no login. Everything you type — names, classes, schedules, attendance, payments, and any photo you add for a person — is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to us or to any server.

No sharing, no ads, no tracking

We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit your data. The app contains no advertisements and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Your control & backups

You can edit or delete any entry at any time. The app includes a Backup option so you can save a copy of your data to a file, and Restore it later or on another device — those files stay wherever you choose to keep them. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device.

Children’s data

Any information about children is entered by a parent or guardian for private use on their own device, and is only shown back to them within the app.
